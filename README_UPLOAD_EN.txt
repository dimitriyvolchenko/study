STUDY v019 · SUDAK · 27 NOVEMBER 2026

Open Study at https://dimitriyvolchenko.github.io/study/ on your existing device.
If an earlier version is still shown, use Cloud > Force update the app or open
https://dimitriyvolchenko.github.io/study/refresh.html. The app keeps the existing profile

SUDAK
Tap white 初, yellow 固, red 習 or green 憶 to change a Word Pack’s stage in place.
When a stage is selected, the other four controls turn gray.
Hold the × for two seconds to clear the mark. Releasing early cancels the reset.
Tap 璃 to open a pack on a black glass screen. Tap it again to return to Sudak.
Hold 십자말풀이 for two seconds to open the crossword for the current Sudak list.
Select a cell or clue and type the answer in its original language. Tap a crossing
again to change direction. New grids fit within 13 columns. A correct answer
advances to the next clue. Previously saved puzzles keep their original grids.
Different languages can share a cell by sound. Selecting a word uses its alphabet.
Use the archive to save a grid and start it again later. Archives belong to the
current profile and are stored on this device.

EXPERTOLOGY
Select a chapter or a study pack in Rocket. Sudak uses the terms and explanations
from that selection. Reading-only chapters and case guides link to their source.

WALKMAN WALLPAPERS
In W, select either Sony Walkman template. Choose one or two body blocks, then
optionally add five words on either side of the clock. Review the full-size image
before saving it for the Lock Screen.

IPHONE WIDGETS
In Sudak, open Виджеты iPhone. The iScreen tab creates a picture to import
into a photo widget. The Scriptable tab provides the script and copies the
current set of words for import into Scriptable. Import the set again after
changing the Rocket selection.

FLAT PACKAGE
All app and course files are at the root of the ZIP. For manual upload, extract
the archive, open https://github.com/dimitriyvolchenko/study on main, and upload
the extracted files together. Existing repository folders and the build workflow
are used alongside the flat files. Family progress files are excluded.
FILES_SHA256.json lists the release checksums.

COURSES
The flat package manifest includes the French and family courses and both
expertology projects currently registered in the repository. The original project
imports STUDY_Pooh_Expertology_FULL.project.json and SE2001_ALL_21_PACKS_ONE_IMPORT.json
are also included.
